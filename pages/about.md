## Purpose

The Dissertation Collection Demo is a teaching collection about the organizational adoption of generative artificial intelligence in leading academic libraries in China and the United States. 
It demonstrates how a researcher can organize theoretical, empirical, methodological, and policy resources with CollectionBuilder-GH.

## Collection Scope

The initial collection contains four foundational books and may grow to include scholarly articles, policy documents, reports, authorized videos, and other lawful online resources. 
It is a practice collection rather than a comprehensive systematic-review corpus.

## Metadata Design

Standard CollectionBuilder fields support item display, search, maps, timelines, subjects, locations, and data downloads. 
Project-specific fields document resource type, dissertation usage, theoretical framework, geographic focus, and access status.

## Rights and Access

Books and restricted articles appear as metadata-only records linked to lawful catalog, DOI, publisher, or institutional pages. 
The site does not host copyrighted full text or cover art without permission. 
Rights information applies to each described resource and should be reviewed before an item is embedded or downloaded.
